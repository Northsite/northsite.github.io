Questions

Before you book

What documents do I need for a self-drive rental?

A valid driving licence held for at least one year, plus Aadhaar or passport for address proof. We photograph both at handover and return them immediately.

How is an outstation trip billed?

Outstation is charged on actual kilometres run, with a minimum of 250 km per day, plus a ₹400 per night driver allowance. Tolls and state permits are billed at cost with receipts.

Can you pick the car up for detailing?

Yes. Anywhere in Surat city we collect and return at no extra charge for Full Detail and Ceramic Coating bookings.

How long does ceramic coating take?

Two days. The paint correction happens on day one and the coating needs to cure overnight before we hand the car back.

Do you have cars available at short notice?

Usually yes, especially hatchbacks and sedans. Send your dates on WhatsApp and we will confirm availability within the hour during working hours.
